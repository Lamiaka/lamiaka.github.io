How engagements work

  2. Build

    Six to eight weeks on one outcome: a measurement programme running, an evaluation harness in production, a model your team owns.

  3. Continue

    Optional monthly support with capped days and a written response window, for teams who want a senior partner on call.

Questions

Before we talk

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes. I sign a standard NDA before looking at any data, and your data stays in your systems unless we agree otherwise.

Do you work remotely or on site?

Mostly remotely, with on-site sessions in the Zürich area when working side by side helps, for example to kick off a project or hand it over.

What do you need from us?

One person who owns the question, read access to the relevant data, and two short check-ins a week while the project runs.

Which tools do you work with?

Yours. I work in Python and SQL on the warehouse and cloud you already use, so everything keeps running after the engagement ends.

What size of company do you work with?

I work best with teams where data decisions carry real money but there is no large data science team yet, and with larger teams that need a senior specialist for one well-defined problem.

What happens after the first call?

If there is a fit, I send a one-page proposal for the diagnostic with scope, dates and a fixed fee. Nothing starts until you have agreed to it in writing.
