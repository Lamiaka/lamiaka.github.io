Short bio

I started in physics: an MSc in Applied Physics at EPFL, then a PhD at ETH Zürich measuring how electrons leave matter on attosecond timescales. The lab taught me one habit I still rely on every day: a measurement is not a result until you have ruled out that you are measuring your own apparatus.

At eBay I brought that habit to business data over eight years and four roles, from data analyst in growth marketing to data scientist in eBay's Core AI group. Five of those years were in the global growth marketing organisation, working on experimentation, attribution, incrementality and personalisation. From 2024 I worked on eBay's conversational shopping assistant, taking it from capability evaluation to production.

Today I offer my data and AI expertise through my consultancy, Kasmi Data & AI. I work from Zürich, in English, French and German.
